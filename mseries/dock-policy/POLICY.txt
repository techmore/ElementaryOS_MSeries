Dock attention and approval policy — user instruction, 2026-10-18

The user reported that the floating dock repeatedly steals attention and prevents typing.
The user explicitly requested that the dock be killed, kept from returning, and that this
incident be documented. Keep the dock disabled unless the user explicitly approves
re-enabling it through the GUI approval process below.

Before changing dock appearance, style, position, focus or attention behavior, launcher,
installed dock binary, or enablement, prepare the exact change and use:
  /home/sdolbec/.local/bin/mseries-dock-change 'Concrete proposed change' -- command arguments

The GUI must show the proposed change and exact command. Apply authorizes that command
once. Cancel is the default. A 90-second timeout, closed dialog, missing GUI, or GUI error
must cancel without executing the change. Do not treat silence or elapsed time as approval.
Do not silently remove the disabled marker, restore the old launcher, launch a dock binary
directly, change the session's dock PATH, or reinstall over the guard. Installer/revert
operations must preserve this policy and the disabled state unless explicitly approved.
Keep automatic startup quiet: do not open approval dialogs during login or automatic
relaunch. A deliberate request to review a prepared change is the only reason to open one.

The marker and launcher guard implement disabled startup. The approval helper gates commands
that are run through it; it is not an operating-system access control. Independent programs
and installers can bypass or overwrite local files. Do not claim an absolute lifetime guarantee.
