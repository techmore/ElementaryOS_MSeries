M1 Pro features and Omarchy parity

What the installed elementary OS 9 desktop does on a 14-inch MacBook Pro (M1 Pro), checked against the maintained Omarchy Apple Silicon packages (omacom/omarchy-mac-pkgs, audited at 222f7a8). This describes the installed system, not the unbuilt factory image. Updated 2026-10-14.

Desktop additions

  • Network indicator: live download and upload speed with a 60-second graph, per-app and per-host usage, and a quieter Wi-Fi list. A background logger keeps the usage history locally. Built into the Apple Silicon image overlay.
  • Local Troubleshooter: plain-language answers about crashes and failures from an on-device model, with a dropdown in the panel. Nothing leaves the machine.
  • Login speed: the greeter went from 119.7 s to 0.82 s with a display crossbar in the initramfs.
  • Look and feel: rounded screen corners, softer blue and rose accents, and a brightness slider in quick settings.
  • Performance: zram swap, trimmed services and a battery CPU policy. Benchmark scripts and results are in mseries/benchmarks; no new speed claim is made without a measured before and after.
