The live driver guards names/images/resource limits/destinations and verifies creation tokens before cleanup. It rejects a non-Wayland backend before waiting, stops if its window is closed, samples resources every five seconds and exits unsuccessfully on workflow or cleanup failures. Python garbage is collected at phase checkpoints. The independent probe is synthetic and never calls Incus. Scope and exclusions

The first pilot inherited GDK_BACKEND=x11; its results are excluded from the native comparison. A separate long-running installed-window observation is labeled XWayland in the evidence. Interrupted candidate pilots were also excluded; one was closed, and another was superseded by the dialog signal fix after completing its workflow cycles. The final native runs explicitly select Wayland and preserve their source hashes.

This qualifies the observed session. Multi-hour/day stability, controlled battery or thermal effects, huge fleets, other themes/scales, interactive upload/download picker selection and Console input, and the separate Vala manager/panel remain unqualified. No kernel, bootloader, audio or global display settings were changed.
