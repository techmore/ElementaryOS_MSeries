The October 21 attended trial booted 7.2.10-elementary-aurora72-wip2; Aurora .20 is the last documented persistent boot default. These are separate tested builds, not interchangeable qualification. The later passive wip3 release observation does not transfer the wip2 hardware results. Exact hardware receipts cover boot/PMP/idle accounting plus separate GPU and filesystem smoke tests; full release qualification remains incomplete. This is an installed transformed system, not our mkosi factory image. The factory ISO built successfully on October 13 but has not been booted on hardware. See the desktop notes and bug-fix audit.
