Support & parity matrix

Hardware evidence: elementary OS 9 Early Access boots to Pantheon on a 2021 14-inch MacBook Pro (MacBookPro18,3, M1 Pro). As of 2026-10-18, the running kernel is Aurora .20, 7.1.12-2-20261017.20-sep-ARCH, with its matched J314S boot payload. Exact hardware receipts cover boot/PMP/idle accounting plus separate GPU and filesystem smoke tests; full release qualification remains incomplete. This is an installed transformed system, not our mkosi factory image. The factory ISO built successfully on October 13 but has not been booted on hardware. See the desktop notes and bug-fix audit.

Rows retain their evidence dates. October 14 results describe the earlier kernel and do not automatically qualify Aurora .20. The current freeze is experimental.
